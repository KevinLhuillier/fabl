Privacy policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy explains what data the Taleway mobile application (“the app”) collects, why, and what your rights are.

Data controller

Kevin Lhuillier, sole proprietor (France), SIRET 901 311 902 00026, 62 allée de la Corolle, 59650 Villeneuve-d'Ascq, France.
Contact: taleway.app@gmail.com

Data collected

Data Why
Email address and password (stored encrypted) Create your account, sign you in, reset your password
Username Identify you in the app
Chosen language and daily goal Show the interface and translations in your language, track your goal
Reading progress (stories, chapters, pages) Resume reading where you left off
Words looked up and review results Build your “My words” list and schedule your reviews
Practice time, streaks and points Compute your daily goal and your streak
Premium subscription (plan, status, expiry date, purchase history) Unlock premium content on your account, on all your devices

Payments are processed by Google Play: we never have access to your bank details.

Legal basis: performance of the service you request by creating an account or subscribing (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad trackers.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for commercial purposes.
  • No access to your camera, contacts or location.

Microphone and reading aloud

The microphone is used only when you start reading a page aloud, and only while it is active. Your voice is turned into text by your phone's speech recognition service (Google on Android, Apple on iOS), which may process it on its servers under its own privacy policy. Taleway only receives the recognized text, uses it on your phone to compare your reading with the page, and keeps neither the recording nor the text.

Processors

  • Supabase: database hosting and authentication.
  • RevenueCat (United States): subscription management. Receives your account's technical identifier and your purchase history, never your email or reading data.
  • Google Play: subscription payments, under Google's privacy policy.

Data may be transferred outside the European Union; such transfers are covered by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Retention

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. When the account is deleted, it is erased within 30 days at most. Purchase history may be kept longer by Google Play and RevenueCat to meet their accounting and legal obligations.

Your rights

You can access, correct or erase your data, object to its processing or request its portability by writing to taleway.app@gmail.com. To delete your account, see this page. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority, such as the CNIL in France (cnil.fr).

Security

Communication between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Each user can only access their own account's data.

Changes

This policy may change. The date at the top of the page shows the latest version. If the translations differ, the French version prevails.
