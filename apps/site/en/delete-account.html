Delete my Taleway account

From the app

  1. Open Taleway and go to the Profile tab (Profil in French).
  2. Tap Delete my account (Supprimer mon compte) at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Confirm: your account and your data are erased immediately.

By email

If you no longer have access to the app:

  1. Send an email to taleway.app@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Taleway account”.
  2. Send it from your Taleway account's email address (or include that address in the message).
  3. You will receive a confirmation once the deletion is done.

Subscription

Deleting your account does not cancel an active subscription: cancel it first in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions).

Data deleted

All data linked to your account is permanently erased:

  • email address, password and username;
  • language and daily goal;
  • reading progress;
  • “My words” list and review history;
  • practice time, streaks and points;
  • your subscription status.

Purchase history may be kept by Google Play and RevenueCat to meet their accounting and legal obligations.

Timeframe

From the app, deletion is immediate. By email, it is done within 30 days at most. No other data is kept afterwards.
