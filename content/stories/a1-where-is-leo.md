# Leo Is Not Here

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a1-where-is-leo/chapter_1.page_1-1792280813937.mp3
It is Saturday morning.
Mia has a cat. His name is Leo.
Leo is black and white. He is small and very cute.
Every morning, Leo eats in the kitchen with Mia.
But today, Leo does not come.
Mia is worried. "Where is Leo?"

---

Mia goes into the garden.
The grass is green and the sun is hot.
She looks under the table. No cat.
She looks behind the flowers. No cat.
"Leo! Leo!" she calls. Nothing.

---

Mia goes out into the street.
There are many cars and a big red bus.
She looks under the cars. No cat.
She asks a boy, "Do you see a black and white cat?"
"No, sorry," the boy says.

# Mr Green

Then Mia sees her neighbour, Mr Green.
Mr Green is old and very kind. He has a big brown dog.
"Hello, Mia! Are you OK?" he asks.
"No. I can't find my cat, Leo."
"A black and white cat? I walk my dog in the park every morning."
"Today, there is a black and white cat in the park. It is near the big tree."
"Thank you, Mr Green!"

---

Mia runs to the park.
The park is big and green.
Children play and dogs run on the grass.
Mia sees the big tree. She looks up.
Leo is in the tree! He is very high.
"Meow! Meow!" Leo is scared. He can't come down.

# A New Friend

Mia wants to climb the tree, but it is too high.
She looks around. She sees a young man. He is very, very tall!
"Excuse me! Can you help me, please? My cat is in the tree."
"Of course!" says the man. His name is Sam.
Sam stands under the tree. He puts his long arms up.
He takes Leo. It is easy!

---

"Thank you, Sam!" says Mia. Leo is happy too. "Meow!"
Sam smiles. "I have a cat too. Her name is Luna."
"Do you want a hot chocolate at my house?" Mia asks.
"Yes, please!"

---

Mia, Sam and Leo walk home together.
In the kitchen, Mia gives Leo his food.
Leo eats. Then he sleeps on her bed.
Today, Mia finds her cat... and a new friend.
"Good night, Leo. No more trees, OK?"
