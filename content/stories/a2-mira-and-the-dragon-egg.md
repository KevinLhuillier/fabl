# The Blue Egg

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_1.page_1-1793035260641.mp3
Mira is eleven years old. She lives with her grandmother in a small village called Ashvale. The village is next to a big, dark forest. People call it the Whisper Forest, because the trees make a soft noise when the wind blows.
Every morning, Mira goes into the forest with a basket. She looks for mushrooms and berries. Her grandmother sells them at the market on Saturdays. Mira knows the forest very well. She is never afraid.
One morning in autumn, she walked further than usual. She arrived at a very old tree. It was enormous, and its roots were as big as snakes. Between two roots, she saw something strange. It was round and blue, and it was shining a little.

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_1.page_2-1793035272546.mp3
Mira moved closer. It was an egg, but it was bigger than her two hands. She touched it carefully. It was warm, very warm, like a cup of tea.
"Hello?" she said. Nobody answered. She looked around. There was no nest and no bird. Only the trees, the wind and the blue egg.
"I can't leave you here," Mira said. "A fox is going to eat you." She put the egg in her basket, under the mushrooms, and she walked home quickly.
At home, she didn't show the egg to her grandmother. She hid it in the barn, in the hay, next to the old cow. That night, she couldn't sleep. She was thinking about the egg. What was inside?

# Ember

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_2.page_1-1793035282823.mp3
Three days later, Mira went to the barn very early. She heard a small noise: crack, crack, crack. The egg was moving! A line appeared on the blue shell, then another line.
Suddenly, a little head came out. It had two big golden eyes, two small horns and a long nose. Then came a body with blue scales, four short legs and two tiny wings.
Mira put her hand on her mouth. "A dragon," she whispered. "A real dragon."
The baby dragon looked at her. It made a sound like a cat. Then it sneezed, and a little spark flew out of its nose. The spark landed in the hay. Mira quickly put her foot on it.

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_2.page_2-1793035294772.mp3
"Careful!" she said, laughing. "You're going to burn the barn. I'm going to call you Ember."
Ember was hungry. He didn't want milk, and he didn't want bread. But he loved apples. He ate six apples in five minutes, and then he fell asleep in Mira's arms. He was warm and heavy, like a stone in the sun.
Mira was happy, but she was also worried. In Ashvale, everybody was afraid of dragons. In the old stories, dragons burned houses and stole sheep. There was a rule in the village: "If you see a dragon, ring the bell."
"Nobody can know about you," Mira told the sleeping dragon. "It's our secret."

# A Big Secret

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_3.page_1-1793035306286.mp3
Dragons grow fast. After two weeks, Ember was as big as a dog. After a month, he was as big as a pony. The barn was too small for him now, and the old cow was not happy.
Every night, Mira took Ember into the forest. He ran between the trees and tried to fly. He opened his wings, jumped and fell. He jumped again and fell again. But every night, he flew a little higher.
Mira's grandmother noticed things. "Where are all my apples?" she asked. "And why do you smell like smoke?" Mira didn't answer. She just smiled.

One evening, there was a problem. Ember was playing near the barn, and he sneezed. This time, it wasn't a small spark. It was a big orange flame. The haystack behind the barn caught fire.
Mira ran to the well with a bucket. Ember was scared and hid behind the barn. Then the neighbours arrived with more buckets. They put out the fire, but it took a long time.
Tom, the baker's son, was there too. He was twelve, and he always asked too many questions. He walked behind the barn, and he stopped. His face became white.

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_3.page_3-1793035324055.mp3
"Mira," he said quietly. "Is that... a dragon?"
Mira took his arm. "Please, Tom. Don't tell anybody. He's not dangerous. He's only a baby. He just sneezes a lot."
Tom looked at Ember. Ember looked at Tom, and then he gently pushed his head against Tom's hand. Tom laughed nervously. "OK," he said. "I promise. But you can't hide a dragon forever."

# The Shadow in the Sky

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_4.page_1-1793035333809.mp3
Tom was right. A week later, something terrible happened. It was market day, and the square was full of people. Suddenly, the sky became dark. Everybody looked up.
A huge shadow was flying over the village. It was a dragon, but not a small one. It was enormous, bigger than the church. Its scales were blue, like Ember's scales, and its wings made a noise like thunder.
Someone rang the bell. People screamed and ran into their houses. The mayor shouted, "Get your bows! Get your spears! The dragon is going to destroy Ashvale!"

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_4.page_2-1793035342971.mp3
But the big dragon didn't attack. It flew in circles above the forest, and it called. Its voice was long and sad. It sounded like a question.
In the barn, Ember heard the voice. He lifted his head and answered with a small cry. Mira understood immediately. "That's your mother," she said. "She's looking for you."
Mira's grandmother opened the barn door. She saw Ember, and she saw Mira's face. For a long moment, she said nothing. Then she sat down on a box.

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_4.page_3-1793035351256.mp3
"So that's where my apples went," she said. Mira started to cry. "I'm sorry, Grandma. I wanted to protect him."
Her grandmother took her hand. "I know. But now the men have bows and spears. If the mother dragon comes to the village, people are going to get hurt. People, and dragons too."
Mira dried her tears. "Then we must take Ember to her," she said. "Tonight. Before it's too late."

# Going Home

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_5.page_1-1793035360267.mp3
That night, Mira, Tom and Ember walked into the Whisper Forest. Grandmother stayed in the village. "I'm going to talk to the mayor," she said. "Old people are good at talking."
They walked for hours. The forest was dark and cold, but Ember's breath made a little light. At midnight, they arrived at the old tree, the place where Mira found the egg.
The mother dragon was there. She was waiting between the trees. Her eyes were golden and as big as windows. Tom hid behind Mira. Mira's legs were shaking, but she didn't run.

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_5.page_2-1793035369823.mp3
Ember ran to his mother. He jumped around her, and she touched him with her long nose. She made a deep, warm sound, like a cat but a thousand times bigger.
Then the mother dragon looked at Mira. She lowered her enormous head until it was in front of the girl. Mira didn't know what to do, so she said, "He likes apples. And please be careful when he sneezes."
The dragon closed her eyes slowly, like a thank you. Then a blue scale fell from her neck and landed at Mira's feet. It was as big as a plate, and it was warm.

---

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/a2-mira-and-the-dragon-egg/chapter_5.page_3-1793035381950.mp3
Ember came back to Mira one last time. He put his head on her shoulder. "Goodbye, Ember," she whispered. "Don't forget me." Then the two dragons opened their wings and flew away, over the trees, towards the mountains.
When Mira and Tom came back to Ashvale, the sun was rising. The men put down their bows. Grandmother was smiling. "The mayor listened to me," she said. "In the end."
Mira hung the blue scale above her bed. In winter, it kept her room warm. And every year, on the first night of spring, people in Ashvale see a small blue light in the sky above the mountains. It comes close to the village, it goes round three times, and then it goes home. Mira always leaves a basket of apples near the old tree. In the morning, the basket is always empty.
