# The Window

@audio https://wvmjobeketinbzxggzuq.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/audio/b1-pistache-above-the-bakery/chapter_1.page_1-1792684975052.mp3
My name is Pistache. I am a ginger cat with one white sock on my left paw, and I live on the third floor of an old building in Lyon, just above a bakery. My human is called Léa. She is a baker, so she gets up when the whole city is still asleep.
Every night at three o'clock, her alarm rings. Léa groans, puts on her slippers and goes downstairs to make bread. I stay in bed for a few more minutes, because the blanket is still warm. Then I go to my favourite place in the world: the kitchen window.

From the window, I can see everything. I can see the street, the rooftops and the chimneys, and far away, the lights of the city. At that time of night, the street is silent. Only the smell of fresh bread climbs up the walls and comes in through the window. It smells like butter and home.
But the window is not just a place to watch the world. It is also where I keep my secrets. For example, I know that the man in the ground-floor flat across the street never opens his curtains. His name is written on the letterbox: Albert Morel. I have seen him only twice. He is old, he walks slowly, and he always looks at the ground.

My other secret is more exciting. Every night, a small grey cat appears on the roof opposite. She walks along the edge, between two chimneys, as if the roof belonged to her. She has no collar. Her tail is long and thin, and one of her ears has a little cut at the top.
Sometimes she stops and looks straight at me. I look back. Neither of us moves. Then she jumps onto another roof and disappears into the dark.
I have never been outside. Léa says the city is too dangerous for a cat like me. "You're an indoor cat, Pistache," she tells me, scratching my head. "You have everything you need here."
She is right, I suppose. I have a bowl that is never empty, a sofa, a sunny spot in the afternoon and a human who loves me. But when the grey cat runs across the rooftops, my paws feel strange. They feel like they want to follow her.
